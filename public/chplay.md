```
📱 ThinkSave — Save articles, read offline. Anytime. Anywhere.

ThinkSave lets you save web articles from Chrome, Facebook, Telegram, or any app and read them later — even without internet. Perfect for commutes, flights, or areas with poor connectivity.

✨ KEY FEATURES

🔖 Save from Any App
Share any URL to ThinkSave — it instantly saves the article for offline reading. Works with Chrome, Facebook, Telegram, Twitter, and more.

📖 Clean Offline Reader
Enjoy a distraction-free reading experience with adjustable font sizes and light/dark themes. No ads in the article view — just pure content.

⬇️ Smart Download Engine
Articles are downloaded in the background with images. ThinkSave intelligently extracts the main content, stripping ads and clutter.

🔍 Quick Search
Find any saved article instantly by title or domain name.

📊 Reading Progress
ThinkSave remembers where you left off. See your reading progress at a glance on the home screen.

🌐 Multi-Language Support
Available in English, Vietnamese, and Chinese (简体中文). Switch languages in Settings.

📤 Share & Export
Share saved articles with friends or open them in your browser anytime.

📊 Offline Status Dashboard
Track the status of each article — Queued, Downloading, Processing, or Ready — so you always know what's available offline.

🛡️ PRIVACY FIRST

• All content is stored locally on YOUR device
• No account required — no sign-up, no cloud sync
• No personal data is collected or transmitted
• Your reading library stays 100% private

📱 PERFECT FOR

• 🚇 Commuters — read articles on the subway without signal
• ✈️ Travelers — save articles before your flight
• 📚 Students — collect research articles for later
• 💼 Professionals — save industry news for offline review
• 🌍 Anyone in areas with unreliable internet

💡 HOW IT WORKS

1. Find an article in any app
2. Tap Share → ThinkSave
3. The article downloads in the background
4. Read it anytime — no internet needed

📋 STATUS INDICATORS

• 🟡 Queued — Waiting to download
• 🔵 Downloading — Actively fetching content
• ⚙️ Processing — Extracting article content
• ✅ Ready — Available for offline reading
• ❌ Failed — Could not download (tap to retry)

🌍 LANGUAGES

• English
• Tiếng Việt
• 简体中文

📱 DEVICE REQUIREMENTS

• Android 5.0 (API 21) or higher
• Internet connection required for downloading articles
• Reading saved articles works fully offline

ThinkSave — Your personal offline reading library. Save it. Read it. Anywhere.
```
